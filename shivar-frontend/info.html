How it works

From your field to the buyer, in simple steps.

Choose your role and sign in

Pick Farmer, Buyer or Logistics, then log in or register with email verification.

List or discover crops

Farmers list crop, quantity, price and location. Buyers browse what is available.

Match, bid and negotiate

Smart matching suggests suitable partners. Bid and chat directly to agree on a deal.

Arrange delivery

Logistics partners pick up the produce and deliver it to the buyer.

Common questions

Quick answers before you start.

Who can use Shivar?

Farmers, industries and bulk buyers, and logistics partners. You choose your role when you sign in.

Are price forecasts guaranteed?

No. Forecasts are estimates to support your decisions, not guaranteed prices or financial advice.

Is the AI advisory a replacement for an expert?

No. It is a decision-support tool. Please confirm important decisions with a qualified agricultural professional.

Should I verify government scheme details?

Yes. Always check scheme information against official government sources before applying.
