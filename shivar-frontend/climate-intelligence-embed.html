Build Note

This page runs on live public weather and climate data (Open-Meteo) for whatever location is searched — forecast, soil moisture, evapotranspiration, and 14-day rainfall history. The three risk scores are a transparent rule-based index computed in the browser from that data and the selected crop stage, not a trained model — it feeds the same Shivar AI Decision Engine that Market and Ecosystem Intelligence use, and will move server-side as the crop-stage and district calendars grow.
